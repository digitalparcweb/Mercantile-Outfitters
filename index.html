Still made the same way it started.

Mercantile started as a single rack of tees in the back of a gear shop, the kind of shirt you bought because it'd survive the drive home, not because of the logo on it. Everything we make still follows that rule.

No warehouse, no committee. Just a Minnesota guy who got tired of tees that didn't hold up.

The FounderMercantile Outfitters, Minnesota
"We didn't set out to make the perfect travel tee. We set out to make the one we'd actually reach for on the next trip."
From the Founders

Three things we get right.

01

Fabric

Combed, ring-spun cotton at 6.5–7.2oz, heavier than most, so it drapes instead of clings and outlasts a normal tee by years, not months.

02

Fit

Three fits, one philosophy: cut for movement. Classic Crew, Relaxed, and Heavyweight Pocket. Pick the one that matches how you actually move.

03

Finish

Every badge is screen-printed or embroidered in small batches, pulled from real topo lines, compass roses, and range silhouettes, not clip art.
